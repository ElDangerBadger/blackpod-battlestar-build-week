and posture reversals, opposing defensive/cyclical rank relationships,
unsupported MA or named-sector claims, unsupported aggregate price direction,
specific market-causal/forward-certainty assertions and temporal comparisons
without prior evidence. Checks run on generation, saved-brief reuse and reader
publication. Conditional scenarios and supported uncertainty explanations remain
valid; rejected prose is not repaired, published or retried.

passed 189 cases, including 25 semantic regressions, with zero skips. One retained
September 18 brief passed the finite checks read-only; it was neither regenerated
nor rewritten. This is one narrative check, not a predictive-quality sample.

replacement or an execution instruction source.

BlackPod is ready for **operator-supervised, explicitly scoped shadow research
recording and review**: hypotheses can be identified, challenged, reproduced,
classified and retained without authorizing an order. It is **not yet ready for
unattended end-to-end effectiveness evaluation, strategy promotion or execution**.
No empirically supported real-market alpha or investment advantage is established.

The existing readers can explain retained evidence. A fresh reader check is not
fresh market research or present ModelDock health. The retained Sentry checkpoint
was read through its four pinned files during this sprint: `READY` reader status,
all tested policies failed the joint criteria, and zero completed prospective

### G. Genuine remaining blockers

1. Current real Trading research still lacks independently admitted action
   coverage, applicable historical feed/size and cost/capacity assumptions,
   approved simulation risk and an uncertainty/stability protocol. All 26
   symbol/action-type cells and eight native reasons remain unresolved.
2. Forecast effectiveness needs predeclared, source-linked subsequent outcomes
   and an appropriate versioned evaluator/baselines. Existing intake explicitly
   forbids invented outcomes. No paired evaluation sample exists here.
3. Investment economic support needs defined fundamental/valuation observations,
   release-time evidence, independent challenge and horizon monitoring. Retained
   retrospective demonstrations and deadline fixtures do not supply it.
4. Oracle's finite guards require continued human review for broader semantics.
   Structured/lexical validity cannot establish unsupported causes or forecasts.
5. Sentry's frozen prospective protocol remains blocked by retained historical
   vintage/calendar issues. Reader refresh cannot resolve or restart it.

These are evidence and validation blockers, not a call for another agent
hierarchy. No automatic capital redistribution, new trading rules or next
coverage-admission burst is implemented in this sprint.

## Readiness and recommended next milestone

Trading can reproduce specified descriptive offline simulations, but credible
economic validation on the retained real history remains blocked by evidence
qualification and uncertainty/stability methodology. Investment can evaluate
specification and exact deadline evidence while keeping thesis support separate
from realized performance; independent economic meaning, valuation applicability
and causal/horizon coverage remain missing. The journal now links assessments,
supersession and later native outcomes without rewriting original decisions.

training-only cost/capacity calibration, plus a frozen uncertainty/stability
protocol before economic evaluation. Gate completion on deterministic source
binding, chronological availability, unchanged holdout, applicable calibrated
cost evidence and explicit simulation-risk review. This recommendation is not
implemented here. Investment fundamental/valuation adapters remain a separately
governed subsequent milestone.
